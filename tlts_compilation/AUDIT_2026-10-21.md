# Audit of the reachability-masking result (2026-10-21)

Re-ran `supplementary/experiment_loci_comparison.py` and `experiment_cyclic_stress.py` from a clean copy.

## Finding: the (B') numbers depended on Python's string-hash seed
Variant (B') built its admissible label set as a Python `set` of strings and sampled from a dict built by iterating it.
String hashing is randomised per process (PYTHONHASHSEED), so the order of the labels, and with it the mapping from the
numpy draw to a label, changed between processes. The numpy seed (42) did not pin the result. Five processes with the
same seed gave (B') = 62.5-64.5% (good prior) and 4.2-5.6% (bad prior). The published 61.5% / 4.3% is one such draw, and
the acyclic arm of the cyclic stress test (65.0%) is another draw of the same quantity, which is why the two tables disagreed.
Variants A, C and D were not affected at the reported precision.

Fix: iterate sorted label sets (four lines in `experiment_loci_comparison.py`). Results are now identical under any hash
seed (checked with PYTHONHASHSEED 0, 1, 5, 7). `supplementary/audit_rerun_reachability.py` reports 10 numpy seeds (42-51):

| ontology | prior | A (no enforcement) | B' (reachability mask) | D (per-step check) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| acyclic | good | 48.3% (45.2-49.6) | **63.3% (60.8-65.1)** | 100% |
| acyclic | bad | 4.5% (3.4-5.4) | **4.5% (3.3-5.6)** | 100% |
| cyclic | good | 6.2% (4.8-8.8) | **6.6% (5.7-7.3)** | 100% |
| cyclic | bad | 0.0% | 0.0% | 100% |

## What changes in the claims
- Unchanged: reachability masking helps a well-calibrated model (+15 points), does nothing for a mis-calibrated one, and
  collapses to no enforcement when the ontology has a cycle (+0.4 points); the per-step check gives 100% throughout.
- Corrected numbers: 61.5% -> 63.3% (range 60.8-65.1 over 10 seeds); 4.2 -> 4.3% becomes "4.5% with or without";
  cyclic 6.9 -> 8.0% becomes 6.2 -> 6.6%; the "advantage falls from 17 points to 1" becomes "from 15 points to under 1".
- `main.tex` / `main.pdf` (self-published preprint) still carry the single-draw numbers. They are not edited here because
