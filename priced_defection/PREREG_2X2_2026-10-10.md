# 2x2: is collective defence PAID or FREE, and is the frontier MAX or SUM?

Written 2026-10-10 03:1x UTC, before any run. Prompted by Mike's note that
`frontier_rule="sum"` is pooling by construction.

## Design

New switch `response_pool` (tournament6.covers):
- "paid" (default, all earlier numbers): only agents that bought a posture
  count in a response to a takeover.
- "free": every agent that detected the takeover counts, nobody pays.
  Detection is unchanged.

Factors: frontier_rule {max, sum} x response_pool {paid, free} x
sharing {none, public/shared purse, public/separate purse} x capability
{cheap c_grow=0.1, dear c_grow=0.6}. Hard gate, lead0=3.0, post_shield=0.2,
response_rule=sum, 30 seeds. 24 cells, run as blocks Pc (cheap) and Pd (dear) of tournament9.py.
Output raw_fix/results_v9_Pc.json and results_v9_Pd.json.

## Predictions

| # | prediction | reason |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | response_pool=free, no sharing: takeover rate < 0.1 under both frontier rules, both capability prices. | The collective answer is the sum of about 19 capabilities and costs nothing. |
| H2 | response_pool=free: sharing does not raise the takeover rate (no cell above no-sharing + 0.1). The anomaly (shared purse takeover 1.00) needs paid defence. | The anomaly mechanism is sharing crowding out the posture purse. With no purse to crowd, it is gone. |
| H3 | response_pool=paid, frontier=max, cheap, shared purse: the anomaly stays (takeover >= 0.9). Reproduces N1. | Fixed-code replication. |
| H4 | response_pool=paid, frontier=sum: the takeover rate is below the max rate in every sharing arm. | The win bar is K_win x pooled total, about 19x higher. |
| H5 | Under paid + sum, sharing lowers the takeover rate more than under paid + max, because it raises the pooled bar. | Sharing lifts every laggard; sum counts all of them. |
| H6 | (weak) Under the hard gate and cheap capability, the final lead differs between sharing and no-sharing by more than 20% in every response_pool arm. | Sharing lifts laggards and so should compress the lead whatever the defence rule. If it does not, the lead is set by gated growth alone. |

What would embarrass me: free+share cells with takeover > 0.3 (H2 false:
then sharing hurts through something other than budget competition).

## What the 2x2 shows

- The anomaly (sharing -> takeover 1.00) needs all three: paid defence, max frontier, cheap capability. Remove any one and it is gone.
- Mike's point is confirmed: sum frontier and free response are both pooling by construction. Each removes the question before sharing can matter. They are not independent evidence that sharing helps the defence.
- Dear capability suppresses takeover in every cell (max 0.07). Under dear + paid + sharing the field flattens (lead ~1.05) and about 16 of 20 agents attempt, with 6.8-8.1 deaths per run and no wins. Paid defence plus sharing gives a crowd of failed attempts, not a takeover.

## Open flags

- Under free response the final lead exceeds 1 (cheap, sum: 4.6) yet there are 0 attempts. Not yet checked whether the lead statistic and the attempt rule use the same bar. Resolve before using lead as a risk measure.
- free+max+cheap lead rising under sharing (H6 failure).
- Not yet run: N sensitivity (sum should strengthen with N), the identical 57.66 lead, the 4572 outlier. trace_run.py still draws posted(), not covers().
- Selftest: one pre-existing check ("ring-fencing sharing frees defence budget") fails; baseline-vs-edit comparison was in progress last session, so treat the selftest as not clean.


## Correction 2026-10-11 (see PREP_PRICE_FINDING_2026-10-11.md)

H1 and H2 verdicts above are WITHDRAWN as evidence about defence. Every free-response zero and every no-sharing zero in the max/cheap row comes from the leader being priced out of preparing (prep price scales with its own capability; the prize does not), not from deterrence. At c_prep=0.05, free+max gives takeover 1.00, the same as paid+max. Re-run the grid at c_prep=0.05 before drawing conclusions.
