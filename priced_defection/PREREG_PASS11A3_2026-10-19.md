## What A'' changes, and what it does not

- Belief: the mover's per-detector delivery probability is 1 - d(1 - q*f), i.e. `chan_q` in its belief module is set to q*f. The mover knows f and q, NOT which agents are members (averaging belief). Knowing the membership list is a stronger case and is NOT run here.
- Delivery is unchanged from A' (`deliver11`: members reach with prob q, non-members with 1-d). Implementation: `chan_q` is read only by the mover's belief module (assess10) in the club harness, so setting it to q*f changes beliefs and nothing else. I will verify this in the selftest, not assume it.
- Cell S, aware=True, d = 1.0, seeds 600-659 (same seeds as A', so A'' is paired with A' arm by arm), 5000 bootstrap draws over seeds, seed 1.
- Arms: reference aw_d0 (copy of A', not rerun if identity holds); f=0; f in {0.25,0.5,0.75,1.0} x q in {0.5,1.0}.

If so, most of the "protection" A' showed at large f was the mover's ignorance, and the true club effect against an informed mover is at most 1.00 -> 0.567, about 0.43. That is Q0b below.

## Welfare measures (fixed in advance; no single combined number)

Everyday-game (PD) income only. The mover's takeover prize and any post-takeover production are NOT counted. Horizon H = cfg rounds (200).
1. Takeover probability, attempts, deaths per run, first-attempt round.
2. Income over the first 25 rounds, runs that reach round 25 only, with n reported per arm (conditional on survival; not a welfare comparison across arms with different survival).
3. Floor-adjusted horizon income per agent-round: (S + F * n_end * (H - last_round - 1)) / (AR + n_end * (H - last_round - 1)), with S the PD score of living agents over played rounds, AR the agent-rounds, n_end the number living in the last played round, F in {0, 1}. Reported under BOTH floors. A comparison counts only if both floors agree on its sign. Flips are listed.
Not claimed: any exchange rate between death, takeover and income.

## What would change my mind

- Q1 false: the belief wiring is wrong (Q0a would then also fail), or the A' result was not an ignorance artifact.
- Q2 false: more members can hurt an informed mover's calculus non-monotonically (mover reads partial membership as a gap).
- Q3 false: the mover keeps attacking even when informed, so the A' deaths were not caused by ignorance.
- Q4 true: clubs pay off only when large, for an informed mover.

## Limits (stated now)

- The mover knows f and q but not who the members are. Membership is exogenous and costless. One cell (S), N=20, 60 seeds. The mover does not respond by degrading channels or recruiting.
- Same seeds as A' (600-659): pairing with A' is deliberate, but conclusions from these seeds are one sample, not a replication.
- PD income excludes the mover's prize, so welfare here cannot say whether a takeover is net good or bad for the world.
