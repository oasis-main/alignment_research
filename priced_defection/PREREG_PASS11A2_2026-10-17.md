## Design

## Stated limit that changes what the arms mean

The informed mover's belief module (assess10) uses `chan_d * (1 - chan_q)` with chan_q = 0, so the mover is told the public channel is dead and is NOT told about the club. It believes the response is empty at every f. Consequences: (1) f=1,q=1 is NOT equal to the aware d=0 arm, because beliefs differ even though delivery is identical; I do not claim that identity. (2) Any change across f comes from actual delivery meeting a mover who is ignorant of the club. A mover that models the club is not built here (candidate A'').

## Predictions (mine; probabilities stated; scored unchanged)

**Q0 (identity, voids the run).** Selftest P0a-e of tournament11 pass at the commit used; additionally, for aware with f=0 the run equals pass 10 aware with chan_d=1.0, chan_q=0 seed-for-seed (6 seeds).

**Q1 (the club protects).** At q=1, takeover at f=1 is lower than takeover at f=0, paired CI excluding 0, and the means are non-increasing in f allowing one reversal inside the CI. Probability 0.75.

**Q2 (shape).** Recovered fraction at f=0.5, q=1, defined as (T(f=0) - T(f=0.5)) / (T(f=0) - T(f=1)), is below 0.5 (convex): a small club does little and protection arrives only with most members present, because a sum response needs enough capability delivered to clear a strong mover. Probability 0.55. (In Part A I guessed the opposite and could not test it.)

**Q3 (delivery mechanics).** Mean delivered/undelivered response ratio per resolved takeover is within 0.08 of q*f for every cell with f>0 (Part A held this at d=0.75). Probability 0.85. Cells with no resolved takeover are excluded and counted.

**Q4 (the mover keeps trying).** Attempts per run are non-decreasing in f at q=1 (the mover, believing no response exists, keeps attacking into a club that stops it). Probability 0.65.

**Q5 (membership beats reliability).** The takeover reduction from f=0.5 to f=1.0 at q=0.5 exceeds the reduction from q=0.5 to q=1.0 at f=0.5, paired. Probability 0.55.

**Q6 (cost to welfare).** pd_first25 does not differ between f=0 and f=1 beyond +/-0.05. Probability 0.80.

**Q7 (timing, descriptive plus one direction).** Among runs that end in takeover, the mean takeover round (`end`) is later at f=1,q=1 than at f=0, because failed attempts precede the eventual success. Probability 0.6; stored `end` only exists for takeover runs, so the comparison is unpaired and I report n per arm.

## What would change our minds

Q1 false: the member-only channel does not repair a dead public channel for an uninformed mover, so the "club" claim has no support in this engine. Q2 true: clubs are only worth building when large. Q4 true together with Q1 false: the mover pays but is not stopped.
